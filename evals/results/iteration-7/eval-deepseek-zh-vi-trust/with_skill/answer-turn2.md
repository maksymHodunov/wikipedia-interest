### Key Findings

**In Chinese (中文):**
- ChatGPT has 4× the traffic of DeepSeek (30.7K vs 7.5K views/month)
- Both declining, but ChatGPT's decline is gradual (−39% share/year, noisy trend) while DeepSeek is sharp (−78% trend, post-launch collapse)
- ChatGPT trust is high (75); DeepSeek trust is only medium (55)

**In Vietnamese (Tiếng Việt):**
- ChatGPT is **growing (+43% share/year)** with high trust (85)
- DeepSeek is **declining sharply (−82%/year)** with low trust (15, unreliable due to 70% bot traffic)
- ChatGPT has 18× more traffic (9.5K vs 531 views/month) with minimal bots (6% vs 70%)
- Vietnamese Wikipedia readers show sustained interest in ChatGPT, not DeepSeek

### For Your Integration Decision

**Vietnamese market is key:** ChatGPT interest is growing among Vietnamese Wikipedia readers (+43% share), even as platform traffic declined 29%. This signals strong, sustained curiosity about AI assistants in Vietnamese.

DeepSeek shows a classic launch-spike-then-collapse pattern in both markets. It trails ChatGPT by a wide margin and is losing momentum everywhere.

**Recommendation:** If choosing between them for these markets, ChatGPT shows stronger and more sustained interest—especially in Vietnamese. DeepSeek looks like a one-time launch event. Vietnamese interest in AI assistants is real and growing (ChatGPT +43%); it's not shifting to DeepSeek. Before integrating DeepSeek, validate with search trends and direct user research in both markets.
