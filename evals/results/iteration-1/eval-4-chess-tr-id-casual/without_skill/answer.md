Indonesia > Turkey for chess localization.

Indonesia is significantly ahead: it's the 6th largest chess market on Chess.com with 411K+ accounts, experiencing rapid growth driven by content creators and online engagement. Turkey has about 300K accounts and is growing competitively but more slowly.

Indonesia's advantages:
- Larger online player base
- Faster growth in blitz/online formats
- High smartphone adoption fueling digital chess
- Strong content creator ecosystem

Turkey's strength is in competitive/classical chess (producing grandmasters), which is a smaller market segment.

Recommendation: Indonesia is the better bet if you're targeting casual/online players. Turkey could be secondary or part of a broader Balkan/Middle East rollout later.
