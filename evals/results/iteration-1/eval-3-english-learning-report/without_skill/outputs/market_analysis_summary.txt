GERMANY
  Strengths: Highest proficiency, largest market, strong willingness to pay
  Focus:     Professional/business English, advanced learners, premium tier
  Risk:      High competition (Babbel HQ), saturated market
  Strategy:  Position as specialized alternative (e.g., technical English)

POLAND
  Strengths: High proficiency growth, young demographic, large market potential
  Focus:     Affordable, gamified content, young adult segment (16-35)
  Risk:      Price-sensitive market, preference for free models
  Strategy:  Freemium model with community features; target school leavers

FRANCE
  Strengths: Established demand, cultural distinction, large Wikipedia base
  Focus:     Conversational/cultural English, European standards
  Risk:      Strong French language identity may limit appeal
  Strategy:  Culturally-localized content emphasizing French-English code-switching

SPAIN
  Strengths: Large market, mobile-first culture, Spanish content synergies
  Focus:     Business Spanish + English bridge, regional variants
  Risk:      Duolingo already dominant in Spanish market
  Strategy:  Niche focus (e.g., LatAm English variants, business communication)

TURKEY
  Strengths: Largest growth potential, high willingness to adopt new apps
  Focus:     Foundational English, tech/startup vocabulary
  Risk:      Wikipedia blocked (no traditional content distribution), regulations
  Strategy:  App-native content, tech community partnerships, long-term play
